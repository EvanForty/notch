NOTCH is a Solana launchpad where every token launches with a reserve-backed price floor. Trading fees fill the reserve, and each time the reserve crosses its next threshold, the floor steps up a notch and locks there.

1Launch a token

Every token starts with a floor reserve.

Anyone can deploy a token through NOTCH. Liquidity is protocol-controlled, and each token gets its own floor reserve from the first block, so it begins life at N0 with a minimum redemption value already in place.

There’s no presale and no team allocation waiting to unlock. The creator can take a first buy of up to 5% at launch, paying the same fee as everyone else, and it’s shown publicly.

2Generate trading fees

Every buy and sell pays 3%.

3Unlock a notch

The reserve fills, the floor steps up.

Each token has a visible threshold for its next notch. When the reserve reaches it, the token unlocks that notch and its guaranteed redemption floor rises in proportion.

A notch unlocks every time the reserve grows 25% beyond the level locked at the previous notch, raising the floor by 25%.

4Lock the new floor

A floor, once reached, stays.

Each notch becomes the new minimum redemption value, provided the reserve stays fully collateralized and protocol rules block any withdrawal that would undermine the backing.

When holders sell hard, the market price can fall a long way, but redemptions are supported at the floor, and sells keep paying into the reserve on the way down.

5Buyback and burn

Excess reserve buys tokens and burns them.

When a reserve earns more than its backing requirement and predefined conditions are met, the excess buys tokens from the market and burns them permanently. Fewer tokens in circulation means more backing behind each one.

Questions

Can the floor go down?

Under the protocol rules, a locked notch doesn’t step down in SOL terms while the reserve stays fully collateralized. Its value in dollars still moves with the price of SOL.

What is a notch?

A milestone in a token’s backing. Each time its reserve crosses the next threshold, the floor rises and the token moves up a level: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, then unlimited further notches.

What does a swap cost?

3% on every buy and every sell: 1.5% to the token’s floor reserve, 0.5% to $NOTCH buyback and burn, 0.5% to the creator and 0.5% to the treasury.

Is redemption at the floor guaranteed?

Redemptions are supported at the established floor, subject to the reserve’s backing and available liquidity.

Can the creator pull the reserve?

The design gives creators no withdrawal rights over liquidity or the reserve. Creators earn 0.5% of volume and can take a public first buy of up to 5%.

What does $NOTCH do?

It’s the platform token. 0.5% of all volume across every NOTCH token buys $NOTCH from the market and burns it.

Where is the official address?

Only from @NotchPadDotFun on X. Treat any address posted anywhere else as unofficial.

Why Solana?

Fees on every trade only make sense when the underlying transaction is cheap and fast. Solana keeps per-swap costs low enough for small trades.
